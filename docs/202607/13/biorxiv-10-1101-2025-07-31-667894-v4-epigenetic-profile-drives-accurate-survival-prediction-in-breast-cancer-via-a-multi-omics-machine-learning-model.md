## 摘要
准确的总体生存期预测是乳腺癌个体化治疗的关键，但仅依靠突变负荷是不够的。为了提高预后准确性，我们整合了来自802名乳腺癌患者的基因组、转录组、蛋白质组、表观遗传和临床特征，开发了BANDOL（乳腺癌新生物数据与组学学习分析）随机生存森林模型。BANDOL正确预测了73%患者对的生存排序，并优于基于突变的模型（时间依赖AUC：0.9-1 vs. 0.4-0.9）。免疫激活特征与治疗后更长的总体生存期相关，而较短的总体生存期则与TREM2髓系细胞、B细胞和瘦素信号传导有关。该模型的可转移性在三个独立的TCGA队列（来自不同的癌症类型：子宫、卵巢和低级别胶质瘤）中进一步探索，尽管肿瘤间存在生物学差异，但仍保持了中等预测性能。表观遗传特征是BANDOL最强的总体生存期预测因子。当前疗法可与靶向ME3、PPARG、OLIG3和SLC25A22基因甲基化的策略相结合。本研究证明，通过机器学习整合多组学数据可增强生存预测，并揭示可操作的生物标志物。

## Abstract
Accurate overall survival (OS) prediction is key for personalized treatment in breast cancer, but mutation burden alone is insufficient. To improve prognostic accuracy, we integrated genomic, transcriptomic, proteomic, epigenetic, and clinical features from 802 breast cancer patients to develop BANDOL (Breast cancer Analysis with Neoplastic Data and Omics Learning), a Random Survival Forest model. BANDOL correctly predicted survival ranking in 73% of patient pairs and outperformed mutation-based models (time-dependent AUC: 0.9-1 vs. 0.4-0.9). Immune activation signatures correlated with a longer OS after therapy, while a shorter OS was linked to TREM2 myeloid cells, B cells, and leptin signaling. The transferability of the model was further explored in three independent TCGA cohorts from distinct cancer types (uterine, ovarian and lower-grade glioma), where moderate predictive performance was maintained despite biological differences between tumors. Epigenetic features were the strongest OS predictors for BANDOL. Current therapies may be combined with strategies to target the methylations of ME3, PPARG, OLIG3 and SLC25A22 genes. This study demonstrates that multi-omics integration via machine learning enhances survival prediction and reveals actionable biomarkers.
