## 摘要
准确的总体生存（OS）预测是个性化治疗乳腺癌的关键，但仅凭突变负荷是不够的。为提高预后准确性，我们整合了802名乳腺癌患者的基因组、转录组、蛋白质组、表观遗传和临床特征，开发了随机生存森林模型BANDOL（乳腺癌肿瘤数据与组学学习分析）。BANDOL正确预测了75%患者对的生存排序，并优于基于突变的模型（时间依赖性AUC：0.9-1 vs. 0.4-0.9）。免疫激活特征与治疗后更长的OS相关，而更短的OS与TREM2髓系细胞、B细胞和瘦素信号传导有关。该模型的可转移性在两个来自不同癌症类型（子宫和低级别胶质瘤）的独立TCGA队列中进行了进一步探索，尽管肿瘤之间存在生物学差异，但仍保持了中等预测性能。表观遗传特征是BANDOL最强的OS预测因子。当前疗法可与靶向ME3、PPARG、OLIG3和SLC25A22基因甲基化的策略结合。该研究表明，通过机器学习进行多组学整合可增强生存预测并揭示可操作的生物标志物。

## Abstract
Accurate overall survival (OS) prediction is key for personalized treatment in breast cancer, but mutation burden alone is insufficient. To improve prognostic accuracy, we integrated genomic, transcriptomic, proteomic, epigenetic, and clinical features from 802 breast cancer patients to develop BANDOL (Breast cancer Analysis with Neoplastic Data and Omics Learning), a Random Survival Forest model. BANDOL correctly predicted survival ranking in 75% of patient pairs and outperformed mutation-based models (time-dependent AUC: 0.9-1 vs. 0.4-0.9). Immune activation signatures correlated with a longer OS after therapy, while a shorter OS was linked to TREM2 myeloid cells, B cells, and leptin signaling. The transferability of the model was further explored in two independent TCGA cohorts from distinct cancer types (uterine and lower-grade glioma), where moderate predictive performance was maintained despite biological differences between tumors. Epigenetic features were the strongest OS predictors for BANDOL. Current therapies may be combined with strategies to target the methylations of ME3, PPARG, OLIG3 and SLC25A22 genes. This study demonstrates that multi-omics integration via machine learning enhances survival prediction and reveals actionable biomarkers.
