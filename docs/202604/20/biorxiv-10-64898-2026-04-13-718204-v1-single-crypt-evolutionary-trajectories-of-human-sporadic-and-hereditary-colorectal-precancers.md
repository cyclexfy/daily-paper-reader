## 摘要
我们对区分人类散发性与遗传性癌症的最早期基因组过程和演化轨迹的理解仍然有限，这阻碍了早期诊断和风险分层。在本研究中，我们对来自5名散发性患者和3名家族性腺瘤性息肉病（FAP）患者的正常结直肠黏膜、癌前息肉和恶性结直肠癌（CRC）中解剖出的319个单隐窝进行了全基因组测序。我们发现，散发性息肉中66%的癌前隐窝并不携带APC突变，但经常积累非典型但受正向选择的驱动突变，包括KMT2C、CACNA1A和FBLN2。相比之下，FAP中大多数癌前隐窝（71%）确实获得了一个或多个体细胞APC突变或5q染色体杂合性丢失（LOH）。在单隐窝水平上，散发性息肉主要受时钟样（clock-like）突变过程主导。与此相反，FAP隐窝演化出了更多样化的基因组过程，包括活性氧相关的损伤、APOBEC相关的诱变以及pks+大肠杆菌相关的诱变。尽管发病年龄明显更轻，但FAP中的癌前隐窝表现出的LINE-1逆转录转座负荷比散发性隐窝高出2倍。系统发育时间估算显示，FAP中的第一个体细胞APC突变通常发生在出生前。系统地理学分析进一步揭示了早期多克隆息肉中的空间谱系隔离，随后在后期的单克隆息肉中出现了“大爆炸”（Big-Bang）式的谱系混合。总之，这些数据表明早期结直肠肿瘤发生并不遵循通用的“APC优先”模型，散发性和遗传性癌前病变遵循不同的演化轨迹。我们的研究还强调，癌前阶段从多克隆到单克隆的转变代表了结直肠肿瘤的“大爆炸”式增长，为早期诊断和风险分层提供了重要见解。

## Abstract
Our understanding of the earliest genomic processes and evolutionary trajectories that distinguish sporadic from hereditary human cancers remains limited, impeding early diagnosis and risk stratification. Here, we performed whole-genome sequencing of 319 single crypts dissected from normal colorectal mucosa, premalignant polyps and malignant colorectal cancers (CRCs) from five sporadic patients and three familial adenomatous polyposis (FAP) patients. We found 66% of premalignant crypts from sporadic polyps did not harbor APC mutations, but frequently accumulated non-canonical but positively selected driver mutations including KMT2C, CACNA1A and FBLN2. In contrast, most premalignant crypts (71%) in FAP indeed acquired one or more somatic APC mutations or 5q loss of heterogeneity (LOH). Sporadic polyps were dominated by clock-like mutational processes at single-crypt level. In contrast, FAP crypts evolved more diverse genomics processes, including reactive oxygen species-associated damages, APOBEC-associated mutagenesis and pks+ Escherichia coli-associated mutagenesis. Premalignant crypts in FAP also exhibited a 2-fold higher burden of LINE-1 retrotranspositions than sporadic ones, despite arising at a substantially younger age. Phylogenetic timing estimations showed that the first somatic APC mutation in FAP often occurs prenatally. Phylogeographic analysis further revealed spatial lineage segregation in early polyclonal polyps, followed by Big-Bang lineage intermixing in later monoclonal ones. Together, these data demonstrate that early colorectal tumorigenesis does not obey a universal APC-first model, where sporadic and hereditary precancers follow distinct evolutionary trajectories. Our study also highlights that polyclonal-to-monoclonal transition in premalignant stages represents the Big-Bang growth of colorectal tumors, offering crucial insight into early diagnosis and risk stratification.
