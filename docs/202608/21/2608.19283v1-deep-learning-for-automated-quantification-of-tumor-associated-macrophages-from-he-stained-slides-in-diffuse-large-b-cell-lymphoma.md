## 摘要
虽然M2极化的肿瘤相关巨噬细胞（TAMs）已被确定为弥漫大B细胞淋巴瘤（DLBCL）疾病侵袭性的指标，但传统的CD163免疫组织化学（IHC）仍然耗费资源。本研究旨在探讨使用深度学习直接从标准苏木精-伊红染色（HE）组织切片中定量TAMs的可行性。利用一个包含52例DLBCL患者的高分辨率HE图像和IHC验证标注（1,713个TAM实例）的精选数据集，评估了五种架构：U-Net、Swin-U-Net、Cerberus-U-Net3+、YOLOv11和HoVer-Net。高CD163 TAM密度（>20.04%）与总生存期显著缩短（HR 2.73; 95% CI:1.21-6.16; p=0.012）和无进展生存期缩短（HR 2.88; 95% CI: 1.23-6.76; p=0.011）相关。在调整IPI、分子亚型（按Hans算法的GCB/non-GCB）、EBV状态和年龄的多变量Cox比例风险分析中，CD163 TAM密度显示出总生存期的预后趋势（HR 3.24; 95% CI: 0.94-11.15; p=0.062）和无进展生存期的趋势（HR 2.41; 95% CI: 0.80-7.32; p=0.120）。在评估的模型中，领域特定的Cerberus-U-Net3+实现了最高的灵敏度（召回率0.656），而基于Transformer的Swin-U-Net展示了优越的分割保真度（精确率0.694，F1分数0.633）。此外，基于预测的Swin-U-Net CD163水平的生存分析揭示了高和低CD163水平患者的20.8%截止点，接近IHC，以及预测CD163值较高的患者总生存期下降趋势为2.63（95% CI: 0.85-8.33; p=0.083）。这些发现表明，使用移位窗口自注意力的深度学习架构有潜力作为IHC的可扩展且成本效益高的替代方案，用于DLBCL中TAMs的预后评估。

## Abstract
While M2-polarized tumor-associated macrophages (TAMs) have been established as indicators of disease aggressiveness in diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL), traditional CD163 immunohistochemistry (IHC) remains resource-intensive. This study aims to investigate the feasibility of using deep learning to quantify TAMs directly from standard hematoxylin and eosin-stained (HE) tissue sections. Using a curated dataset of 52 patients with DLBCL, with high-resolution HE images and IHC-validated annotations (1,713 TAM instances), five architectures were evaluated: U-Net, Swin-U-Net, Cerberus-U-Net3+, YOLOv11, and HoVer-Net. High CD163 TAM density (>20.04%) was associated with significantly reduced overall survival (HR 2.73; 95% CI:1.21-6.16; p=0.012) and progression-free survival (HR 2.88; 95% CI: 1.23-6.76; p=0.011). In multivariate Cox proportional hazards analysis adjusting for IPI, molecular subtype (GCB/non-GCB per Hans algorithm), EBV status, and age, CD163 TAM density showed a prognostic trend for overall survival (HR 3.24; 95% CI: 0.94-11.15; p=0.062) and progression-free survival (HR 2.41; 95% CI: 0.80-7.32; p=0.120). Among the evaluated models, the domain-specific Cerberus-U-Net3+ achieved the highest sensitivity (Recall 0.656), while the Transformer-based Swin-U-Net demonstrated superior segmentation fidelity (Precision 0.694, F1-score 0.633). Additionally, the survival analysis based on the predicted Swin-U-Net CD163 level revealed a 20.8% cutoff point for patients with high and low CD163 levels near IHC, as well as a downward trend in overall survival among patients with higher predicted CD163 values of 2.63 (95% CI: 0.85-8.33; p=0.083). These findings suggest that deep learning architectures using shifted-window self-attention show potential as a candidate surrogate for IHC that is scalable and cost-effective for prognostic assessment of TAMs in DLBCL.
